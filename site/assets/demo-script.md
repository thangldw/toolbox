# Toolbox 45-second Demo Script

[English](#english) · [Tiếng Việt](#tiếng-việt) · [日本語](#日本語)

## English

Use the exact ad-hoc-signed, unnotarized `v2.0.0` public release with a disposable, harmless installer fixture and disposable developer-project fixtures. Keep all private paths out of frame. Do not present the build as Developer ID-signed, Apple-notarized, stapled, Gatekeeper-approved, or physically verified on Intel.

| Time | Screen action | Voiceover |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Open Home, then Change Timeline. | “Toolbox shows what changed on your Mac before you decide what to clean.” |
| 5–12s | Drop the harmless fixture into Install Trace and start. | “Drop a DMG, PKG, or app. Toolbox saves a local before snapshot, then opens the normal macOS installer flow.” |
| 12–20s | Complete the harmless install and press Finish Trace. | “After installation, it compares filesystem metadata and FSEvents—no document contents or telemetry.” |
| 20–28s | Expand one created path and choose Review in Storage. | “Every path keeps its evidence. Reviewing never selects or deletes it automatically.” |
| 28–36s | Open Projects, select one disposable artifact, and confirm Move to Trash. | “Known rebuildable output appears only inside roots I selected, and cleanup is revalidated at confirmation.” |
| 36–43s | Open Recovery, select the action, show the checks, and restore. | “Trash paths stay linked, and restore refuses to overwrite an existing destination.” |
| 43–45s | Return to the Home privacy line. | “Local-first, open source, and built for review before action.” |

### Recording contract

- Capture at `1270×760` or `2540×1520`; final duration is 30–60 seconds.
- Use only representative local fixture data. Show no names, private paths, personal files, notifications, accounts, secrets, or unrelated windows.
- Keep the cursor readable and every confirmation/revalidation step visible; do not use a jump cut to hide a mutation decision.
- If first launch is shown, keep the expected Gatekeeper rejection and **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** flow intact. Never disable Gatekeeper or remove quarantine attributes.
- End on the local-only privacy boundary. The recording demonstrates workflows, not users, downloads, adoption, Apple approval, or physical Intel execution.

Không mô tả build là Developer ID-signed, Apple-notarized, stapled, Gatekeeper-approved hay đã verify trên máy Intel vật lý.

| Thời gian | Thao tác trên màn hình | Voiceover |

| 12–20s | Hoàn tất cài đặt vô hại và bấm Finish Trace. | “Sau cài đặt, ứng dụng so sánh filesystem metadata và FSEvents—không đọc document content hay gửi telemetry.” |
| 20–28s | Mở rộng một created path và chọn Review in Storage. | “Mỗi path giữ evidence. Review không bao giờ tự chọn hoặc tự delete.” |

- Cursor phải dễ đọc; mọi confirmation/revalidation step phải hiển thị; không dùng jump cut để che mutation decision.
- Nếu quay first launch, giữ nguyên Gatekeeper rejection dự kiến và luồng **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**. Không tắt Gatekeeper hoặc xóa quarantine attribute.
- Kết thúc ở local-only privacy boundary. Recording minh họa workflow, không phải evidence về user, download, adoption, Apple approval hay physical Intel execution.
